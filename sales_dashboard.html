What this dashboard is built on

Transparency on data sources and how to unlock the full spec.

This dashboard runs on your Lit Alerts market-intelligence exports (Jan 1 – May 31, 2026): estimated units, price, value, and market share for the entire New Jersey market, sliced by brand, category, dispensary, and day. That makes the Brand Health & Market Traction view — your #1 priority — fully live today: you can see exactly where Growfather, Octane, and Dr. Zodiak stand against 657 competing brands.

✓ Live now (from Lit Alerts)
  • Brand market share, units, value & competitive rank
  • Category share & market structure
  • Dispensary / account landscape & targeting
  • Market daily trend (30/60-day)
  • Premium vs volume positioning
⚠ Needs internal data to activate
  • Stores Added / Lost & Net Store Growth (needs period-over-period)
  • Account revenue, A/R balance, gross profit (Back Office)
  • Days since last order, repeat-order rate (orders data)
  • Revenue vs goal, sales-by-rep (CRM / sales log)
  • Promotion ROI, forecast demand (promo & inventory feeds)
Two data notes:
  • Sugar High and Moonrock had no measurable sales in the NJ market export for this period — likely pre-launch or tracked under a different name. They'll appear automatically once they show market activity.
  • A reliable 90-day trend needs 180 days of history; this export covers 151 days, so only 30- and 60-day trends are shown. The full 90-day view unlocks once a longer export window is available.
